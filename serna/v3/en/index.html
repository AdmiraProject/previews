Rethinking goodbyes, putting families first

At Serna we believe saying goodbye should be personal, dignified and focused on what matters most: honouring a life and being together as a family.

Gilberto Serna López founded the company in 1935, making his own caskets alongside his wife and nine children. Today the fourth generation leads it with the same values.

Today the Serna family keeps building on that legacy: new niche areas, guided farewells with grief specialists and pre-need plans that protect families from rushed decisions.
