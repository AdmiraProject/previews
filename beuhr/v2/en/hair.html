Can I combine an oral and a topical treatment?

Yes, when your doctor says so. In the questionnaire you can choose oral, topical, combined or “let my doctor decide”.

What’s the difference between DENSE and DENSE MAX, and how do I know which one is for me?

DENSE is oral minoxidil (2.5 or 5 mg). DENSE MAX adds finasteride 1 mg in the same capsule to also block DHT.

And between RESTORE and RESTORE MAX?

RESTORE is 5% topical minoxidil. RESTORE MAX adds 0.1% finasteride in a 120 ml spray, with up to 90% lower systemic finasteride absorption than oral.

How do I know it’s working?

Photos. On day 0 we document your starting point, and at months 3 and 6 your doctor compares against it. Six months is the minimum to assess.

Is more shedding at the start normal?

Between weeks 2 and 8 you may see shedding that scares you. It’s a sign of activity, not damage, and your doctor reaches out to support you.

What if I stop treatment?

If you stop, you lose what you gained. Male pattern hair loss isn’t cured, it’s managed, so we remind you before your plan runs out.

How much does it cost?

DENSE from $1,947 MXN and RESTORE from $2,097 MXN. Price is confirmed with your prescription and the assessment is included with your treatment.

Where do you ship?

Anywhere in Mexico, in discreet packaging. Once shipped, it takes 5 to 7 days.
