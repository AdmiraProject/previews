What is BEUHR?

BEUHR stands for Basado En Una Historia Real (Based On A True Story). We’re a Mexican men’s health brand: doctor-reviewed online treatment for hair loss and sexual health, plus supplements for sleep and energy. We talk about what almost nobody talks about, like a good friend would.

How does it work?

You fill out the questionnaire, a doctor reviews your answers and decides whether you’re a candidate and at what dose. If all is well, you get a payment link and your treatment ships discreetly to your door.

Do I need a prescription for everything?

No. Hair treatments (DENSE and RESTORE) and sexual health (APEX) need a medical review. The ZEN, CORE and ASCEND supplements you can buy directly.

How can I pay, and which cards do you take?

With a Visa, Mastercard or American Express debit or credit card, on BEUHR’s official store.

How long does shipping take to my state?

We ship anywhere in Mexico. Once shipped, your order takes 5 to 7 days to arrive.
