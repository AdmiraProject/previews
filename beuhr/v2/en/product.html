Do I need a prescription for this treatment, and how is it decided whether I’m a candidate?

Yes. DENSE and RESTORE are prescription only, so before any prescription you fill out the online questionnaire: basic details, medical and family history, your type of hair loss and your habits. A licensed doctor reviews your answers within 24 business hours and decides whether you’re a candidate, which product fits you and at what dose. If you’re not, they tell you clearly and suggest another path.

When will I see changes?

Every case is different. By month 3 shedding usually slows, and at month 6 we make the first honest comparison against your day-zero photo.

Is more shedding at the start normal?

Between weeks 2 and 8 you may see synchronized shedding. It’s a sign of activity, not damage; your doctor reaches out to support you.

What if I stop using it?

If you stop, you lose what you gained. Male pattern hair loss isn’t cured, it’s managed.

Can I combine it with another treatment?

Only if your doctor says so. In the questionnaire you can ask for oral, topical, combined or let your doctor decide.

How do I pay?

Once your doctor approves your prescription you get a payment link. We take Visa, Mastercard and American Express.

How long does shipping take?

We ship anywhere in Mexico in discreet packaging; 5 to 7 days once shipped.

What’s the difference between DENSE and DENSE MAX?

DENSE is oral minoxidil (2.5 or 5 mg). DENSE MAX adds finasteride 1 mg in the same capsule. Your doctor decides which one is for you.

And between RESTORE and RESTORE MAX?

RESTORE is 5% topical minoxidil. RESTORE MAX adds 0.1% finasteride in a 120 ml spray.

What if I’m not a candidate?

Your doctor tells you clearly and points you to another path. No prescription, no payment link.

How do I know it’s working?

With photos: on day 0 we document your starting point, and at months 3 and 6 your doctor compares against that photo.

What does BEUHR mean?

Basado En Una Historia Real (based on a true story). We’re a Mexican men’s health brand based in Zamora, Michoacán.
