Your protocol, month by month

  1. 01

    Day 0

    The photo

    What you’ll see: Nothing yet. We document your starting point.

    What we do: A guided baseline photo so you can compare later.

  2. 02

    Weeks 2–8

    The shedding that scares you

    What you’ll see: More hair than usual on the pillow, in the shower and in the brush.

    What we do: Your doctor reaches out to you to confirm it’s expected.

  3. 03

    Month 3

    It slows down

    What you’ll see: Shedding drops back to normal levels.

    What we do: First check-in: photos, tolerance and adjustments at no extra cost.

  4. 04

    Months 4–6

    New hair shows up

    What you’ll see: Fine, short, light hairs in the affected areas.

    What we do: Photo tracking against your day zero.

  5. 05

    Month 6

    The first honest comparison

    What you’ll see: A measurable difference between today’s photo and day zero.

    What we do: Formal review: on track, or time to adjust.

  6. 06

    Month 12

    The peak

    What you’ll see: The highest density this plan can give you.

    What we do: We rework the plan with you for maintenance.

  7. 07

    Month 13+

    Keeping it is a choice

    What you’ll see: Keep going and you keep it. Male pattern hair loss isn’t cured, it’s managed.

    What we do: We remind you before your plan runs out.
