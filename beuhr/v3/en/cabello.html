Hair loss: understand why it happens and treat it with a doctor on your side

About hair loss

Androgenetic alopecia is hair loss with a hereditary and hormonal cause: DHT, an androgen your body makes, shrinks the follicle and hair grows back thinner and shorter each time. It usually shows at the temples, the crown or both, and it almost always progresses slowly. It isn’t cured, it’s controlled, so treatment is planned for the long term, with follow-up. About hair loss

What androgenetic alopecia is

It’s the most common form of hair loss in men. It has a hereditary and a hormonal component: DHT, an androgen your body makes, shrinks the follicle. With every cycle, hair grows back thinner and shorter until the area looks sparse. It progresses at a different pace for everyone, so your doctor reviews your case and your starting photo before setting any prescription treatment, and follows up with you month by month.

It isn’t a disease that gets cured. It’s controlled, so treatment is planned for the long term, with follow-up: if you stop, you lose what you gained, so your doctor gives you a heads-up before your plan runs out.

How it shows

It almost always starts gradually. You see more hair in the shower or on the pillow, the hairline moves back, or you comb your hair differently to cover a spot.

  • Hairline: recedes at the forehead and temples.
  • Crown: hair thins on top of the head.
  • Both areas: hairline and crown progress at the same time.
  • Diffuse: hair thins all over.

Why it happens

A mix of genetics and hormones. That’s why the questionnaire asks whether your father, brothers or grandparents have hair loss: your family history helps your doctor understand your case.

Not all hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. If it’s sudden, patchy or started after an illness, your doctor may ask for an in-person visit.

Treatments at BEUHR

They are all prescription-only, and your doctor decides which one fits:

  • DENSE 2.5 and DENSE 5: oral minoxidil, one capsule a day.
  • DENSE MAX 2.5 and DENSE MAX 5: oral minoxidil + finasteride 1 mg in one dose.
  • RESTORE: minoxidil 5% topical solution.
  • RESTORE MAX: minoxidil 5% + finasteride 0.1% in a 120 ml spray.

Treatment works while you keep it up

If you stop, you lose what you gained. That’s why from month 12 the goal shifts from gaining to keeping, and your doctor reworks a maintenance plan with you.

Frequently asked questions

Can male pattern baldness be cured?

Androgenetic alopecia isn’t cured, it’s managed. That’s why treatment is long-term, with your doctor’s follow-up.

What causes male pattern baldness?

A mix of heredity and hormones: DHT, an androgen, shrinks the follicle and hair grows back thinner and thinner.

What’s the difference between DENSE and RESTORE?

DENSE is oral: one capsule a day. RESTORE is topical: applied to the scalp. Your doctor decides which fits you, or whether to combine them.

What changes in the MAX version?

DENSE MAX and RESTORE MAX add finasteride to minoxidil: one ingredient blocks DHT and the other activates the follicle.

How long until I notice any change?

In weeks 2 to 8 you may shed more; it’s synchronization and it’s expected. Around month 3 shedding drops back to normal, and month 6 is the minimum for an honest comparison. Every case is different.

Do I need to send photos of my hair?

Yes. On day 0 we document your starting point with a photo: without a baseline photo you won’t know if you improved. Your doctor uses it as the reference at every check-in.

Who reviews my case and follows up with me?

A dedicated doctor: the same person for your whole treatment, with their name and license number from day one. They reply within 24 business hours, or 4 business hours if you report a symptom.

Is it normal to shed more at first?

Yes. Between weeks 2 and 8 you may see more hair on the pillow or in the shower. It’s a sign of activity, not damage, and your doctor reaches out during that stage.

If I stop treatment, will it fall out again?

Yes: if you stop, you lose what you gained. Keeping it is a choice, and we remind you before your plan runs out.

How much does treatment cost?

Hair treatments range from $1,947 MXN (DENSE 2.5) to $2,397 MXN (RESTORE MAX). The final price is confirmed with your prescription.

Can I treat hair loss online?

Yes. You fill out the questionnaire, a doctor reviews your case and, if you’re a candidate, your treatment ships to your door.
