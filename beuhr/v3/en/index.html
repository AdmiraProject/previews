Frequently asked questions

What is BEUHR?

BEUHR stands for Basado En Una Historia Real, “Based On A True Story.” It’s a Mexican online men’s health platform: medical reviews for hair loss and sexual health, and non-prescription supplements for sleep and energy, shipped anywhere in Mexico.

How does the online assessment work?

You fill out the questionnaire, a doctor reviews your answers and decides if you’re a candidate and at what dose. If you are, you get your payment link and your treatment ships to your door.

How long does the doctor take to reply?

We reply within 24 business hours. If you report a symptom, within 4 business hours.

Do I need a prescription for every product?

No. DENSE, RESTORE and APEX are prescription-only and need a medical review. ZEN, ASCEND and CORE are supplements: you buy them directly, no questionnaire.

What if I’m not a candidate?

Your doctor tells you clearly and suggests another path. If your case needs an in-person visit, we’ll help you with the next step.

How do I pay?

With a Visa, Mastercard or American Express debit or credit card. For prescription treatments, the payment link comes after your doctor confirms.

Where do you ship and how long does it take?

We ship anywhere in Mexico. Once shipped, your order takes 5 to 7 days to arrive.

Is my data confidential?

Yes. Privacy is one of our values: your answers are confidential and only your doctor sees them.

Who is my doctor?

A dedicated doctor: the same person for your whole treatment. You know their name and license number from day one.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. You can request your invoice (factura) in the Invoicing section of beuhr.com.

Prescription only. Requires a medical review.
