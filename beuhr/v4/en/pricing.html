BEUHR pricing: common questions

How much is DENSE MAX?

DENSE MAX 2.5 costs $2,097 MXN and DENSE MAX 5 costs $2,187 MXN, per 30-capsule bottle.

What’s the most affordable option?

DENSE 2.5 (oral minoxidil 2.5 mg), at $1,947 MXN. Your doctor decides if it’s right for you.

When do I pay for a prescription treatment?

After your doctor approves it. You get your payment link and place your order online.

Do supplements need a prescription?

No. ASCEND, ZEN and CORE are bought directly: $799 for one jar, $1,398 for two and $1,947 for three.

How long does shipping take?

5 to 7 days once shipped, anywhere in Mexico.

Which cards do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and American Express, debit or credit.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes, on WhatsApp at 351 160 9794 or at contacto@beuhr.com.

What if I’m not a candidate?

Your doctor tells you clearly and suggests another route. We don’t prescribe something that isn’t right for you.

How fast does my doctor reply?

Within 24 business hours of sending your questionnaire. If you report a symptom, within 4 business hours.
