What every guy wants to know...

Do I need a prescription?

Yes, for hair and sexual health. You fill out a questionnaire, a doctor reviews your answers and decides whether you’re a candidate and at what dose. The ZEN, CORE and ASCEND supplements don’t need one.

When will I notice something?

6 months is the minimum time for an honest evaluation. Between weeks 2 and 8 you may shed more than usual: it’s part of the process and your doctor reaches out to explain what’s happening.

How does my order arrive?

In 5 to 7 days once shipped, anywhere in Mexico, in discreet packaging. Pay with a Visa, Mastercard or American Express debit or credit card.
