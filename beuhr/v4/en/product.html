Day 0

The photo

Nothing yet. We document your starting point. What we do: A guided baseline photo so you can compare later.

Weeks 2–8

The shedding that scares you

More hair than usual on the pillow, in the shower and in the brush. What we do: Your doctor reaches out to you to confirm it’s expected.

Month 3

It slows down

Shedding drops back to normal levels. What we do: First check-in: photos, tolerance and adjustments at no extra cost.

Months 4–6

New hair shows up

Fine, short, light hairs in the affected areas. What we do: Photo tracking against your day zero.

Month 6

The first honest comparison

A measurable difference between today’s photo and day zero. What we do: Formal review: on track, or time to adjust.

Month 12

The peak

The highest density this plan can give you. What we do: We rework the plan with you for maintenance.

Month 13+

Keeping it is a choice

Keep going and you keep it. Male pattern hair loss isn’t cured, it’s managed. What we do: We remind you before your plan runs out.
