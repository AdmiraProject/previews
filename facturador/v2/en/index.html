Why pay if SAT’s portal is free?

SAT’s portal issues one invoice at a time. Facturador saves your customers and products, duplicates and schedules invoices, downloads your purchases, stamps your payroll and keeps your books. And if you get stuck, a person answers.

What do I need to start invoicing?

Your RFC (tax ID), your e.firma (SAT digital signature) and your CSD (digital seal certificate). With those you’re invoicing in 5 minutes, and an advisor calls within 10 to help with the first one.

Do I have to drop my accountant?

No. Your accountant can log in to your account and see your CFDIs, your SAT purchase downloads and your reports. If you don’t have one, Facturador Contable assigns you a team.

Can I cancel invoices?

Yes. You cancel CFDIs from the platform, without going into SAT’s portal. For payroll you can also cancel in bulk.

What happens after the 7 free days?

Nothing is charged on its own: we don’t ask for a card and there are no automatic charges. If you liked it, you pick the plan that fits.

Is my information safe?

Our processes are based on the ISO/IEC 27001 standard. We use encryption, secure Microsoft Azure servers and automatic backups, and we keep your CFDIs for more than 5 years.

Can I invoice from my phone?

Yes. Facturador runs in the cloud: use it from your laptop or phone, nothing to install.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit or debit card, OXXO (cash at convenience stores), bank transfer and PayPal.

Can I change plans?

Yes, any time, from your payment portal.

Does one plan cover several RFCs?

One plan covers one RFC. If you handle several, call us at +52 998 872 3293 and we’ll put an option together.
