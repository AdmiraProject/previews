We answer your questions

Why pay if the SAT portal is free?

The portal lets you invoice, but you type everything every time. Facturador saves your customers and products, duplicates and schedules invoices, downloads your purchases from the SAT and runs payroll and accounting in one place. Plus an advisor helps you from day one.

What do I need to start?

Your RFC (tax ID), your e.firma (SAT e-signature) and your CSD (digital seal certificate). With those you start invoicing in 5 minutes. If something's missing, your advisor guides you on the call.

Do I have to drop my accountant?

No. Your accountant can log into your account and work with your real CFDIs and entries, without asking you for files by email.

Can I cancel invoices?

Yes. You cancel your CFDIs from your platform, choosing the reason the SAT requires, without going to the portal.

What happens after the 7 days?

Nothing is charged automatically: we don't ask for a card. When it ends you choose the plan that fits, and you can change it anytime from your payment portal.

Is my information safe?

We work with processes based on the ISO/IEC 27001 standard, encryption, secure Microsoft Azure servers and automatic backups. Your CFDIs are stored for more than 5 years.

Can I invoice from my phone?

Yes. It's a cloud system: you log in from your computer's or your phone's browser, nothing to install.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit or debit card, OXXO (cash at convenience stores), bank transfer and PayPal.

Does one plan cover several RFCs?

Each plan covers one RFC. If you handle several, call us at +52 (998) 872 3293 and we'll guide you.
