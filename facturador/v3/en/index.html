Why pay if the SAT portal is free?

On the portal you type every invoice from scratch. Here the SAT catalogs are preloaded, you duplicate and schedule invoices, your purchases download from the SAT automatically, and real people support you by phone, chat and WhatsApp.

What do I need to get started?

Your RFC (tax ID), your e.firma (SAT electronic signature) and your CSD (digital seal certificate). With those you can start invoicing in 5 minutes, and an advisor walks you through your first invoice.

Do I have to leave my accountant?

No. Your accountant can log in to your account and work with your data. If you don't have one, Facturador Contable assigns you a team.

Can I cancel invoices?

Yes. You cancel your CFDI from your platform, without going into the SAT portal. Payroll also supports bulk cancellation.

What happens after the 7 days?

Nothing automatic: we don't ask for a card, so there are no charges. If you like it, you pick the plan that fits.

Is my data safe?

We follow processes based on the ISO/IEC 27001 standard, with encryption and secure Microsoft Azure servers with automatic backups. Your CFDI are kept for more than 5 years.

Can I invoice from my phone?

Yes. It's a cloud system: use it from your computer or your phone, nothing to install.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit or debit card, OXXO (cash at convenience stores), bank transfer and PayPal.

Can I change plans?

Yes, anytime, from your payment page.

Does one plan cover several RFCs?

Each plan covers one RFC. If you manage several, call us at +52 (998) 872 3293 and we'll look at your case.

Invoicing is complicated?
Not with Facturador.
