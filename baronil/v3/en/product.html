FAQ

How do I order in the Dominican Republic?

Add what you want to the bag and tap “Order on WhatsApp”. We’ll confirm size, availability, home delivery and payment with you.

Do you sell in the US?

Yes. In the US this product is sold on Amazon; shipping and returns are handled by Amazon.

This style is currently sold in the Dominican Republic only. On Amazon US you’ll find Baronil boxer briefs, briefs and undershirts.

Which size should I pick?

Use the size guide on this page. If you’re between sizes, size up for a roomier fit.

How do I wash it?

Machine wash warm with like colors. Use non-chlorine bleach only when needed.

Classic or Stretch?

Classic is combed-cotton rib; Stretch is 95% cotton and 5% spandex, so it molds to your body and moves with you.

Where is it made?

In the Dominican Republic. Puntex knits, dyes and sews our cotton in Santo Domingo Este, from yarn to garment.
