Description
What matters is on the inside

The everyday essential that stays put: combed-cotton rib, plush-lined waistband and a double-stitched, elastic-free hem.

Fabric: 100% combed cotton rib. Designed and made in Santo Domingo by Puntex.

Product Details
  • Plush-lined waistband: soft on the inside
  • Double-stitched hem with no leg elastic
  • Tagless printed label
  • Prewashed, preshrunk combed cotton
  • Made in the DR by Puntex

Sizes: S · M · L · XL  ·  Style code: CBXC1 · BXCC2

Shipping & Orders
How to order

In the Dominican Republic, order on WhatsApp for home delivery.

In the US, Baronil is sold on Amazon, which handles shipping.

Care

Machine wash warm with like colors. Use non-chlorine bleach only when needed.
