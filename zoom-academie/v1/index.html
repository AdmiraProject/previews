FAQ

Do I need prior experience in event planning?

No. The only prerequisite is a passion for organizing events or the drive to launch a project. The program starts from zero and takes you all the way to launch.

Is the program really 100% online?

Yes. Every class is live online in the evening, so you can follow the program from wherever you are.

What is the exact schedule?

Evening classes from 6:30 to 9:30 p.m. ET, twice a week, over 13 weeks. Plan for a few extra hours a week to advance your own event project.

Can I take the program while working full-time?

That is how it was designed: live classes two evenings a week, about six hours in total. If life gets in the way one week, your coaches and the community help you get back on track.

What language are the classes in?

Live classes are taught in English or in French. Your advisor confirms the cohort and the language on your free call.

What are the payment options?

Pay in full or spread the cost over monthly instalments. Your advisor reviews the financing options you qualify for during the free consultation.

What if I don't land my first contract?

Our guarantee: your first contract within 6 months, or your money back. Certain conditions apply; your advisor walks you through them on your call.

Do I receive a certificate?

Yes. On completion you receive the official Zoom Academy certificate for the Event Planning Program, from a school with 19 years of graduates.

What do I leave the program with?

Your completed business or project plan, your web page, your brand identity and finished visuals for ads and socials, your 90-day roadmap with a Google Ads proposal, your complete event project and your certificate.

How much can an event planner earn?

A single wedding bills between $3,000 and $10,000. A corporate contract (gala, conference, product launch) runs $15,000 to $40,000.

What can I do after the program?

Launch your own event business, run your company's events in-house, join an agency or a festival, or freelance as a coordinator on large productions.

When does the next cohort start?

Spots in each cohort are limited. Book your free consultation to get the next start date and reserve yours.
