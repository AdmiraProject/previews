Who is this program for?

For anyone passionate about events who wants to build a career or launch a business. No diploma or prior experience is required: the program starts from zero and takes you all the way to launch, and your admissions call confirms the fit. After graduation you can work as a corporate event planner, festival & concert promoter, event entrepreneur or in-house event manager, or take the next step if you already work in events. That is exactly how it was designed. Classes are live, two evenings a week, about six hours in total. Most of our students work full-time through the entire program. And if life gets in the way one week, your coaches and the community are there to help you get back on track.

FAQs

Do I need experience in event planning?

No. The only prerequisite: a passion for organizing events or the drive to launch a project. The program starts from zero and takes you all the way to launch.

Is the program really 100% online?

Yes. Every class is live online in the evening, so you can follow the program from anywhere in North America.

What language are the classes in?

The live classes of this program are in English. Your advisor confirms your cohort on the free call.

What is the exact schedule?

Evening classes from 6:30 to 9:30 p.m. ET, twice a week, over 13 weeks. Plan for a few extra hours a week to advance your own event project.

Can I take the program while working full-time?

That is exactly how it was designed. Classes are live, two evenings a week, about six hours in total. Most of our students work full-time through the entire program.

Who is eligible?

Anyone passionate about events who wants to build a career or launch a business. No diploma or prior experience is required. Your admissions call confirms the fit.

What are the payment options?

Pay in full or spread the cost over monthly instalments. Several financing options exist, and your advisor reviews the ones you qualify for during the free consultation.

Do I receive a certificate?

Yes. On completion you receive the official Zoom Academy certificate for the Event Planning Program, backed by 19 years of graduates.

What do I actually leave the program with?

A completed business plan, a live web page, your brand creatives, finished visuals for your ads and socials, a Google Ads proposal, your complete event project built in class, and your certificate.

What can I do after the program?

Launch your own event business, work in-house for a company, join an agency, a festival or a non-profit, or freelance as a coordinator on large productions.

How much can an event planner earn?

A single wedding bills between $3,000 and $10,000. A corporate contract — gala, conference, product launch — runs $15,000 to $40,000, and those clients come back every year.

When does the next cohort start?

Spots in each cohort are limited, first come, first served. Book your free consultation to get the next start date and reserve yours.
