          Frequently asked questions

          Do I need event-planning experience?
          No. The program starts from zero and takes you all the way to launch. All you need is a passion for organizing events or the drive to launch a project.
          Is the program really 100% online?
          Yes. Every class is live, online and in the evening, so you can follow the program from wherever you are.
          What is the exact schedule?
          Classes from 6:30 to 9:30 pm ET, twice a week, over 13 weeks: about 6 hours a week.
          What language are the classes in?
          Live classes are in English, and in French in the French-language program.
          What do I leave the program with?
          Your complete event project, the official certificate and a launch kit worth $5,000: business or project plan, a web page built by the team, brand identity, ad and social visuals, a 90-day client roadmap with a Google Ads proposal, outreach scripts and partnership templates.
          Do I receive a certificate?
          Yes. On completion you receive the official Zoom Academy certificate for the Event Planning Program, from a school with 19 years of graduates.
          What are the payment options?
          Pay in full or in monthly instalments. Financing options are reviewed with you on the free call.
          How much can an event planner earn?
          A single wedding bills between $3,000 and $10,000. A corporate contract (gala, conference, product launch) runs $15,000 to $40,000.
          Is there a guarantee?
          Yes: your first contract within 6 months, or your money back. Certain conditions apply.
          When does the next cohort start?
          Spots in each cohort are limited. Book your free 15-minute call to get the next start date and reserve your spot.
